### You were never the one in charge.

Your heart beats without asking. Your eyes shut before you decide to blink. And three times a day, every day, your body issues an order you cannot refuse and lets you choose only the details.

Now look at everything built on top of that.

The rooms that teach you where power sits before anyone in them speaks. The words engineered so that the death of children can be discussed in a budget meeting. The ancient stories still running as operating instructions. The prediction that manufactures its own fulfilment. The guard that works out it can sell the throne it was built to protect. The money that is not a substance. And the debt reset that every ancient kingdom put on the calendar, and no modern one will.

None of it requires a conspiracy. It requires only that enough people carry the same map and act on it.

> Kill a mother and you have not ended one life. You have removed the person who would have installed the next map, which means you have reached forward and altered a generation that has not been born yet. It is the only form of killing that works on the future.

> Which is why the number to watch in any society is not its wealth or its army. It is how many women die giving birth.

Ammon M. Covino spent the first part of his life inside a structure he could not see, and the last two years working out what it was made of. This is the outward half of the argument. Book Two, The Mitochondrial Self, turns the same question inward and asks what the thing receiving the map actually is.
