## Headline finding

The two v4 files are complementary halves of one book, and neither is
publishable alone.

**The ChatGPT v4.2 edit keeps the voice.** It preserves the tagline and the
signature quotables that the editorial review's chapter-furniture plan depends
on: "The body did the work. The mind got the debrief," the hammer-and-house
line, "He was right about everything except the address," "You are not above
this war," "it has never once asked your permission." But its scholarly
apparatus is a shell: the Notes section is a one-paragraph stub with **zero
numbered notes and zero in-text markers**. A book whose method is "trace every
claim to its Alpha" cannot ship with an empty Notes section.

**The fact-check edit keeps the spine.** It carries a complete apparatus:
roughly 31 numbered endnotes with real citations (Miller 1953; Powner, Gerland
& Sutherland 2009; Spang et al. 2015; the July 2026 Minnesota synthetic-cell
paper correctly flagged as a preprint), matched to in-text [n] markers. It adds
a full copyright page carrying the ESTABLISHED / SYNTHESIS / POSTULATE
taxonomy, applies that taxonomy honestly to the book's own thesis, and adds an
Author's Note that draws the evidence/synthesis/postulate boundary — exactly
the "Note on Evidence" furniture the editorial review says Book Two needs for
parity with Book One. It also renames Part Two "Five Bridges Back to the Outer
World" and labels the short chapters "(Bridge to Book One)," implementing the
editorial recommendation for the callback chapters. The cost: it flattened or
cut most of the quotables, and its hedged register drains the opening chapters
(compare its Chapter 1, "Their established role is already large enough
without mythology," with v4.2's "They are the part of the body that has never
stopped being in charge").

## Recommendation

Use the fact-check edit as the structural base — its notes were verified
against its own wording, so its text and apparatus move together — and restore
the voice from the ChatGPT v4.2 edit chapter by chapter, per the standing
one-file-at-a-time rule. Specifically:

1. Adopt from the fact-check edit: the copyright page with the three-level
   claim taxonomy, the Author's Note, the endnotes and in-text markers, the
   "(Bridge to Book One)" labels, and the Index.
2. Restore from v4.2: the epigraph-grade lines listed above, the preface's
   "never once asked your permission" close, and the assertive chapter
   openings, wherever the restored line is framing rather than a checkable
   claim. Where a strong line makes a checkable claim, keep the hedged wording
   that the note was checked against, or add a note.
3. Then apply the editorial review's §7–§8 furniture (per-chapter quotable +
   ~30-word summary). Neither v4 file has it yet.

## House-style violations (both v4 files)

- **Em dashes.** The pair-wide no-em-dash rule (README: "Book Two follows it
  as of version 4.2") is not applied in either file: the v4.2 ChatGPT edit
  contains ~198 em dashes, the fact-check edit ~147. This is the largest
  outstanding mechanical pass.
- **British spelling.** The fact-check edit retains "behaviour" (2×). The
  v4.2 edit is clean on the forms checked.
- **Author name drift.** v4.2 title page says "A. M. Covino"; the fact-check
  edit says "Ammon M. Covino." Book One uses Ammon M. Covino — standardize.
- **Title.** Both v4 files correctly say *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Homo
  Sapiens* (plural). The Drive v3 still says "Homo Sapien" — a further sign it
  is stale; treat it as reference only.
- **Placeholders.** The fact-check edit's copyright page still reads
  "ISBN [TO BE ASSIGNED]".

## Version hygiene

The same collision the editorial review flagged for Book One is starting in
Book Two: a file named v4.2 whose internal Notes are empty, a differently
named PDF of the same generation, and a Drive folder still holding v3 under
three different titles. When the merged manuscript is committed to the Book
Two repository, pick one version number, let the ledger and the file names
agree with it, and retire the v3 Drive copies to reference-only status.
