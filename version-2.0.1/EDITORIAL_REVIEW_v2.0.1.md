# Editorial Review — The Two-Book Set (v2.0.1)

**Books:** The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Homo Sapiens (Book One, .docx) · The Mitochondrial Self (Book Two, .pdf)
**Review date:** 2026-08-23
**Scope:** Cross-book structural parity, continuity and flow, proofreading (141 line-level issues annotated in the two companion review files)

---

## 1. Verdict in Brief

The pair works. The cross-book architecture is genuinely sound: Book Two's preface correctly describes Book One's thirteen-chapter structure, the "Sign, Revisited" interlude points accurately at Book One's Opening, and bridge chapters 12–14 map onto real Book One chapters (The Parasite and the Host, Words Are Spells, The Prophecy Engine). Readers will see one story told in two directions.

What breaks the illusion of one unified work is the **packaging**: the two books currently use different notes systems, different TOC conventions, different chapter-number styles, different dash rules, different index separators, and slightly different Garamonds. Every one of these is fixable without touching the argument.

## 3. Title Pages That Talk to Each Other

Book One's title page already reaches toward Book Two twice — the epigraph ("…finds the mitochondria looking back") and the closing line "Book Two is The Mitochondrial Self." Book Two reaches back only with an unpunctuated run-on line ("Book Two Companion volume to The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Homo Sapiens").

**Recommended mirrored template (both books):**

```
TITLE
Subtitle
BOOK ONE — or — BOOK TWO
Epigraph (each answering the other's)
Ammon M. Covino
Companion line: "Book Two is The Mitochondrial Self." /
                "Book One is The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Homo Sapiens."
```

Book Two needs an epigraph that answers Book One's. Since Book One's epigraph looks inward toward the cell, Book Two's should look outward toward the map — e.g., built from its own line: "The outer maps that shape a person, and the biological systems already running when those maps arrive."

## 4. The Notes Problem (highest priority)

The two books currently notate in incompatible systems, and each system is also internally broken:

- **Book One** promises "numbered notes correspond to markers in the text," then delivers 25 notes headed by chapter labels. The 39 bracketed markers [1]–[25] in the body resolve to nothing; marker [10] lands on the wrong note; marker order is broken in four places; one [22] is orphaned on its own line.
- **Book Two** delivers real numbered endnotes but skips note 11 entirely, cites notes 29–31 before 12–28, and leaves an editorial to-do ("recheck status at proof") inside note 22, which also has no citation data.

**Fix:** adopt one pair-wide system — numbered endnotes grouped by chapter, retaining the small-caps claim labels (ESTABLISHED / SYNTHESIS / POSTULATE) both books already use. Renumber Book Two 1–30 continuously; rebuild Book One's notes as numbered entries matched to its existing markers.

## 5. Continuity and Flow

**Verified handshakes (no action needed):** "thirteen chapters" claims in both prefaces ✅ · Book Two's account of Book One's opening (Socrates, the daimonion) ✅ · Interlude "The Sign, Revisited" ↔ Book One's Opening ✅ · Bridges 12/13/14 ↔ Book One chs. 9/3/7 ✅ · Both index headnotes use the same chapter-reference convention ✅

**Drift and structural wobble to address:**
- Book One, The Prophecy Engine: "The Woman in the Chapter" runs ~6 pages and never returns to the chapter's frame; its opening refers to figures the chapter has not introduced.
- Book One, Chapter Eight: merges the Minab casualty figures into "a hundred and twenty-three children" twice, though its own Chapter Four note says the source-dependent counts "should not be merged."
- Book One: "Loki Law … is ten rules long," but the rules are never listed; also "Loki Law" vs "Loki's Law" naming split.
- Book Two: the megafauna paragraph concedes the losses were mostly herbivores, then concludes human predators were removed.
- Book Two: broken internal cross-reference — "Chapter 5 established … dynein" (Chapter 5 says nothing about the cytoskeleton).
- Book Two: interlude heading "Three Brains, Not One" asserts what the interlude's own conclusion denies.
- Book Two: "(Bridge to Book One)" tags appear in the TOC but not on the chapter openings themselves.
- Book Two: vague pointers ("the companion volume") should name Book One chapters, matching the precision of the index's "full account, Book One" entries.

**Overreach vs. the books' own Reading Key** (both flagged in the annotated files): Book One — Venezuela reserves ranking, Jubilee releases as accomplished history, five unlabeled strong claims. Book Two — "Dopamine is how the stakes get set," HSV "removed" (note supports "experimental"), "two-thirds of the world carries HSV-1" (note says 64% of under-50s), "it was never a metaphor" (contradicted on p. 30), beaver "running identically."

## 6. Prioritized Fix Plan

**P0 — credibility blockers**
1. Rebuild Book Two's TOC: one entry per line, no mid-word breaks.
2. Unify the notes system pair-wide; repair Book One's marker resolution and Book Two's missing note 11 / citation order.
3. Purge Book Two's 147 em dashes (Book One proves the style is achievable).
4. Fix the broken cross-references (Book Two's "Chapter 5" claim; Book One's marker [10]).
5. Reconcile Book One's merged casualty figure with its own sourcing note.

**P1 — one-book-set packaging**
6. Mirror the title pages (§3) and unify copyright boilerplate + Reading Key placement.
7. One chapter-label and part-label convention across both TOCs; fix Book One's four TOC-vs-body subtitle mismatches and add "How to Read This Book" to its TOC.
8. Repair both indexes: same separator, fix alphabetization, unmerge Book One's run-together entries, add Book Two's missing entries, update Book Two's stale headnote.
9. American-English sweep in both books.
10. Typeset Book One to the Book Two template in a single Garamond cut.

**P2 — polish**
11. Book Two epigraph answering Book One's.
12. Add "(Bridge to Book One)" to Book Two chapter openings; name chapters in cross-book pointers.
13. Trim or re-frame the drift passages (§5); remove jacket copy after Book One's index; resolve Afterword's three/five people contradiction.
14. ISBNs at production.
