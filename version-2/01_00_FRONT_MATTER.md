**THE HITCHHIKER’S GUIDE TO THE HOMO SAPIENS**

Nobody handed you a manual. They handed you a map, before you were old enough to ask who drew it, and you have been navigating by it ever since.

This book is about the map. Where it came from. Who benefits from the direction it points. And what is left of you once you can see it.

It moves through the rooms that teach hierarchy before anyone speaks, the words engineered so that atrocity can be discussed in a budget meeting, the ancient stories that still function as operating instructions, the prophecies that write their own fulfilment, the institutions captured from the inside by things that produce nothing, the money that is not a substance, and the debt reset that every ancient kingdom understood and no modern one will schedule.

It is not a conspiracy theory. Nothing in it requires a meeting. It requires only that enough people share a map and act on it.
