## A. THE EPIGRAPH SYSTEM

**A1. Chapter epigraphs.** One quotable under each chapter title, before the first paragraph. Set smaller than the subtitle, italic, centered.

**A2. Running footers.** Book title on the left, that chapter's quotable on the right, small type, below the page number. Changes per chapter.

*Feasibility note:* this requires each chapter to become its own Word section with a linked-to-previous flag turned off. It is straightforward to build and it will roughly double the length of the build script. Worth doing once, at the end, after the text stops moving. Doing it before the text settles means redoing it every version.

## F. THE IMPLEMENTATION CHAPTER

Tabbed, not written. Contents agreed:

- Austin first, then state, then federal. One seat proven, then repeated.
- Day one, day two, day thirty.
- Where campaign money comes from and what it is allowed to buy.
- SVN as the vote-tracking layer. Study **vTaiwan** first; it is the closest working precedent.
- The delegate model, named as such, with Burke's 1774 counter-argument stated and answered rather than ignored.
- **Sortition** as the mechanism that solves the ideology problem, since a randomly selected person has no donors, no party and no career to protect.
- Earmarked public funds on a public ledger. **This is the strongest item and the only one buildable by a single city without changing anything constitutional. Start here.**
- The four objections that must be answered in the chapter: who decides which questions reach the public; what protects a minority under a bound mandate; what happens when a decision cannot wait; and who controls the platform, since whoever runs the forum controls the framing.

**One correction carried into that chapter.** The requirement for a representative with no ideological imprint contradicts Chapter One, which argues no such person exists. The stronger form: do not look for someone without a map, build a seat where having one does not matter. Every other constraint on the list works regardless of what the person believes, and that is what makes them robust.

## H. ORDER OF OPERATIONS

Do not do these in the order listed above. Do them in this order:

1. **D1, D2, D3** — the lines and decisions. Fast, and they affect everything downstream.
2. **B1 to B5, C1 to C3** — text additions. Small, surgical, chapter by chapter.
3. **E1, E2, E3** — structural decisions. Must be settled before the epigraph system is built.
4. **F** — the implementation chapter. New writing, needs its own pass.
5. **G** — sources, as they arrive.
6. **A1, A2, A3** — the epigraph and footer system. **Last.** It touches every page and should be built once, after the text has stopped moving.
